# Performance report

``chunknorris`` has been benchmarked against other PDF parsing and chunking methods, measuring execution time, energy consumption and retrieval accuracy.

The method and results are detailed in our paper: 📄 [ChunkNorris: A High-Performance and Low-Energy Approach to PDF Parsing and Chunking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00010). Give it a read to learn how heuristics can get high-quality retrieval at a fraction of the computing resources of machine learning based methods!
